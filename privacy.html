Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 19, 2026

1. Overview

"Martian Meets Earth Uploader" (the "App") is a private tool used by one person, the owner of the "Martian Meets Earth" series, to upload their own videos to their own accounts on TikTok, YouTube and Instagram. This policy explains what information the App handles.

2. Information the App handles

  • Authorization tokens issued by the platforms after the owner grants access. They are stored only on the owner's own computer and are used only to publish the owner's videos.
  • Basic account information (for example the account name) is read only to confirm which account is connected.
  • Video files and captions created by the owner, which are sent to the platforms to be published. A temporary copy may be stored on a file-hosting service during an upload and is deleted afterwards.

3. What the App does not do

The App does not collect, sell, share or analyze personal data of other people. It does not read followers' data, comments or messages, and it has no users other than its owner. No advertising or tracking tools are used.

4. Retention and deletion

Tokens stay on the owner's computer until the owner deletes them or revokes access. The owner can revoke the App's access at any time in the settings of the connected platform (TikTok, Google or Instagram), after which the stored tokens stop working.

5. Security

Credentials are kept in a local folder that is excluded from version control and is not shared with anyone.

6. Children

The App is not directed at children and does not collect information from children.

7. Changes

This policy may be updated from time to time. The date above shows the latest version.
